ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Artificial intelligence is a branch of computer science that focuses on developing machines capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Machine learning is a major part of artificial intelligence. It allows computers to learn from data and improve their performance without being explicitly programmed for every task.

Artificial intelligence is widely used in healthcare, education, transportation, banking, and industrial automation.

Machine learning algorithms analyze large amounts of data to identify patterns, make predictions, and support decision-making.

Neural networks are computational models inspired by the structure of the human brain. They are commonly used in image recognition, speech processing, and natural language understanding.

The development of artificial intelligence has created opportunities for automation, improved productivity, and faster information processing.

Artificial intelligence systems can process complex information and assist humans in solving problems across different industries.
