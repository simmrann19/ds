WATER POLLUTION

Water pollution occurs when harmful substances enter rivers, lakes, oceans, and groundwater, making the water unsafe for humans and other living organisms.

Industrial waste, agricultural chemicals, plastic materials, and untreated sewage are major sources of water contamination.

Factories sometimes release chemicals and heavy metals into nearby water bodies. These pollutants can damage aquatic ecosystems and affect the health of organisms living in water.

Agricultural activities can also contribute to water pollution. Fertilizers and pesticides may be carried into rivers and lakes through surface runoff.

Contaminated drinking water can spread diseases and create serious public health problems.

Water treatment plants use physical, chemical, and biological processes to remove harmful substances from wastewater.

Reducing plastic waste, treating industrial discharge, and improving sanitation systems are essential measures for protecting freshwater resources.
