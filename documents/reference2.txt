CLIMATE CHANGE

Climate change refers to long-term changes in temperature, rainfall patterns, and other atmospheric conditions on Earth.

Global warming is primarily caused by the increasing concentration of greenhouse gases in the atmosphere. Carbon dioxide and methane are two major greenhouse gases released through human activities.

Burning fossil fuels such as coal, petroleum, and natural gas contributes significantly to rising global temperatures.

Climate change affects agricultural productivity, water availability, biodiversity, and human health.

Rising temperatures can cause glaciers to melt and sea levels to increase. Extreme weather events such as floods, droughts, and heatwaves are also major environmental concerns.

Renewable energy sources such as solar and wind power can reduce dependence on fossil fuels and help control greenhouse gas emissions.

Protecting forests, improving energy efficiency, and adopting sustainable practices are important steps toward reducing the effects of climate change.
