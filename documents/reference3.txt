RENEWABLE ENERGY

Renewable energy is obtained from natural sources that are continuously replenished, including sunlight, wind, water, and geothermal heat.

Solar energy uses photovoltaic panels to convert sunlight into electrical energy. Wind turbines generate electricity by converting the kinetic energy of moving air into mechanical energy.

The use of renewable energy reduces dependence on conventional fossil fuels and helps decrease environmental pollution.

Solar and wind power are important alternatives to traditional electricity generation because they produce electricity with very low direct greenhouse gas emissions during operation.

Renewable energy technologies can support sustainable development while improving long-term energy security.

Climate change is a major reason for the growing adoption of renewable energy. Reducing carbon dioxide emissions is essential for limiting the rise in global temperatures.

Governments and industries are investing in renewable energy infrastructure to create a cleaner and more sustainable energy system.
