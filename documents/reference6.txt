TECHNOLOGY IN EDUCATION

Technology has transformed the education sector by introducing digital tools that improve teaching and learning experiences.

Online learning platforms allow students to access educational materials, attend virtual classes, and complete assignments from different locations.

Digital classrooms use computers, projectors, smart boards, and internet connectivity to make lessons more interactive.

Artificial intelligence is increasingly being used in education to provide personalized learning experiences and automated feedback.

Machine learning algorithms can analyze student performance and identify areas where additional academic support may be required.

Educational technology also provides access to online libraries, recorded lectures, interactive simulations, and digital assessments.

However, unequal access to devices and reliable internet connectivity remains a challenge for many students.

Combining traditional teaching methods with digital learning tools can create a more flexible and effective educational environment.
